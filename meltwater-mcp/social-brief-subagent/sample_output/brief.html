Brief: Launch a social campaign that taps the current trending topics, stories and cultural moments in trail running.
The big idea

First Mile Club

01Strategic summary

The US trail conversation is being reshaped by first-timers and a values-led 'slow trail' movement — and this audience engages with beginner honesty far more than podium coverage. Northwind should own the on-ramp: a community campaign that makes the first trail 50K feel achievable, welcoming and un-intimidating, riding the spring race-season peak.
